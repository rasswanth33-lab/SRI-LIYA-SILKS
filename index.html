Our Story

About Sri Liya Silks

Sri Liya Silks brings generations of textile craftsmanship into one welcoming showroom. From handwoven silk sarees to everyday wear for the whole family, every piece on our shelves is chosen for its quality, comfort and lasting beauty.

Our team takes pride in personal service — helping every customer, from bride to first-time visitor, find the fabric that feels right. Step in, and you'll see why families return to us season after season.

  • 🧵
    Handpicked Quality

    Every saree and fabric is checked for weave, finish and colorfastness before it reaches the shelf.

  • 🤝
    Personal Guidance

    Our team helps you match fabric, color and occasion — not just point you to a shelf.

  • 🏬
    Full Family Range

    From bridal silks to kids’ festive wear, one showroom covers the whole family.
